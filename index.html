    How the score works (out of 100)

    • Rent - 40 points. Full points at or under your budget, falling to 0 at double your budget.
    • Size - 20 points. Full points at 1,200 sq ft or more.
    • Bedrooms - 15 points. 1 bed: 5, 2 beds: 10, 3 beds: 13, 4+ beds: 15.
    • Facilities - 15 points. 3 points each for gas, water, lift, generator and parking.
    • Distance - 10 points. Full points at 0 km, falling to 0 at 10 km.

    The score only ranks the flats you entered, using your own numbers. It is not a valuation - always visit before you decide.
